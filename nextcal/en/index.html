NextCal

NextCal is a free iPhone app that shows the next events in your Calendar as Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a countdown, so you don't miss what's coming up.

What it does

  • As an event approaches, a Live Activity appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island on its own, and goes away by itself once the event starts. No daily taps or opening the app.
  • Each start time gets its own Live Activity with the event name and a countdown. Events that start at the same time are shown together on one card.
  • With notifications allowed, it picks up newly added events even when the app is closed or force-quit (the notifications are silent and unobtrusive).
  • It works with the calendars already in your iPhone's Calendar app (iCloud, Google, and more). You choose which calendars to use and whether to include all-day events.
  • (Version 1.1 and later, optional) You can also connect Google Calendar directly. It is read-only and picks up changes quickly. Event contents still never leave your iPhone.

Privacy

Event contents (titles, locations, attendees, notes) never leave your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
